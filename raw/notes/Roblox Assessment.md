## Factories
Maxime the production capacity of profitable products from the back to the front. If you encounter bottleneck, adjust the later production capacity to the maximum value that can be completed. In fact, the score will not be very low after completing this step. Then see if we can break through the limited production capacity of bottlenecks through some "buffs". Usually, expanding the production capacity will increase some costs, so it is still necessary to pay dattention to whether it has changed from making money to losing money after the revision...

got 200k, the strategy for me was to completely ignore the unprofitable factories while allocating more resources to the most profitable ones. use modifiers to increase number of items produced too.

This was Roblox’s well-known factory optimization game. I had to design a production plan, decide which products to make and in what quantities, while managing raw material allocation. My key strategy was to identify the bottleneck material and then optimize using a formula like output × 2 + input ÷ 2 to maximize throughput.

At the end, I submitted my score (I got 180k points) along with a short write-up explaining my approach.

There was also a factory pipeline simulation, where I had limited time to optimize a production line by experimenting with different setups. My final score depended on the most profitable configuration I achieved.
